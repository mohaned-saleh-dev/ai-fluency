What could move these dates

Dependency

The QA scenario can't be written yet

Trainer and Content both have drafts. QA has none — the baseline questions about what they review, against what rubric, and what happens after a score are still unanswered.

If those answers don't come out of the validation session, the QA scenario slips and only two roles reach the pilot.

Open question

Does the Trainer scenario match the real job?

The draft assumes trainers build their own material under deadline pressure, but the questionnaire says instructional designers own that. Ask directly in the session.

If the answer is no, switch to the delivery-side version: an agent asks a question the deck doesn't cover and the trainer reaches for AI live.

Accepted risk

The pilot runs before the secure deployment

Deliberate, to protect the pilot date. Until cutover on 17 Sep, real Care responses sit on the current setup — so keep participation to named pilot users and hold off on any wider rollout.

Scope note

3rd-party tool assessment stays a spike

Observe how pilot participants describe using tools beyond ChatGPT, then design the capability from real language. Keeping it out of the pilot scope protects the validity of what the pilot is actually testing.
